# Privacy Policy

**Deed to Map: Property Lines** (called *Deed to Map: Metes & Bounds* before version 2.0)

Effective date: 25 November 2026
Developer: Tigran Barseghyan
Contact: **developer.barseghyan@gmail.com**

---

## In short

The app does not collect your personal data, does not store it on any server, and does not sell or share it. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

The documents you photograph, the parcels you draw, the pins you mark and their photos stay on your device. Three things use the network, all of them Apple's: the map, the address lookups described below, and the App Store purchase.

---

## The documents you photograph

This is the most sensitive thing the app touches, so it comes first.

A deed or survey typically carries names, an address, book and page references, and sometimes a price. When you photograph or import one:

- the images are stored inside the app's own container on your device
- the recognized text is stored with them, on your device
- **neither is ever uploaded, transmitted or seen by the developer**
- deleting the parcel deletes its pages; deleting the app deletes everything

If you choose to email a document to support because it did not read correctly, that is your decision and your message. The app never sends anything on its own.

## Text recognition

Reading the text on your document is done **entirely on your device**, using the text recognition built into iOS. No image and no text is sent anywhere for recognition. This is true for every document, every time, with or without a network connection.

## Location

Location is used:

- to show where you are relative to the parcel and to guide you toward a corner
- to set the point of beginning by standing on it
- to average the position of a pin or corner marker you found, for 30 seconds to 2 minutes
- to guide you along a boundary line, with the distance to go and which side of the line you are on

The app asks for location *While Using the App*, never *Always*.

**In the background.** Location is used in the background only while the **Walk the line** screen is open, so that the distance and side stay right with the phone locked or in a pocket. iOS shows its location indicator meanwhile. It stops when you leave that screen.

**The Live Activity.** During a walk the distance and side are shown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. They are worked out on the device and drawn by iOS. No push server is involved, and nothing about the walk is sent anywhere.

Your location is not sent to the developer and is not stored on any server. Positions you save, such as a pin's, stay on your device unless you export or share them. Apple's map services are the exception, and only as described below: the map loads the area you are looking at, which while you walk is where you are, and a placed parcel's position is looked up to show its address. If you placed the parcel by standing on a corner, or tied it to a pin you stood on, that position is close to where you stood.

You can refuse location access and still read documents, compute parcels and place them by hand. If you turn off *Precise Location*, the app says so and cannot guide you to a corner.

## Pins and their photos

When you mark a pin, the app keeps its position and how good it is, the date, the kind of marker, your notes, and a photo if you add one. All of it is stored inside the app's own container on your device. A photo you add is stored with the pin's position and the time written into the photo file, so that it can serve as a record.

None of this is uploaded. It leaves the device only if you share a pin, or export a KML, GPX, CSV or PDF file, and it then goes where you send it. Deleting the parcel deletes its pins and photos.

## Apple's maps and address search

Four things are sent to Apple's services, and only these. Apple's privacy policy governs them.

**Map imagery.** Satellite and map tiles are served by Apple Maps, which means the area you are looking at is requested from Apple's servers, as in any map application.

**The placed parcel's address.** When a parcel is shown on the map, the coordinate of its center is sent so the app can show a one-line address under the map, and you can confirm the parcel landed in the right place.

**An address or road name on your document.** When a parcel is placed from an address or road name found on your document, or you choose that address in *Find address*, that text is sent to find a position.

**What you type in *Find address*.** While you type, the text so far is sent to Apple so it can suggest places, together with the part of the map you are looking at, or the United States as a whole before the parcel is placed. The suggestion or text you choose is sent to find its position. The last five places you found are kept on your device, for the next time.

None of these carries your identity, your document's images, or anything else the app has stored. If you place the parcel from printed coordinates, from your GPS position or by hand on the map, no text from your document is sent.

## Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. The developer never receives your payment details. The app asks Apple whether a purchase exists; Apple's answer contains no personal information.

## What is stored on your device

- the parcels you create and their calls
- the document pages you photographed or imported, and the text recognized from them
- where you placed each parcel and how you rotated it
- the pins you mark, with their positions, notes and photos
- the measurements you save, and any notes you add to individual calls
- the last five places found in *Find address*
- your settings, such as display units and bearing format

All of it is local. None of it is backed up to any service belonging to the developer. If you use iCloud Backup, your device backup is covered by Apple's terms, not by this policy.

## Exports and sharing

Exporting to KML, GPX, CSV or PDF, and sharing a report, are actions you take. The file goes where you send it. The developer receives nothing.

## What the app does not do

No user accounts. No analytics or crash reporting services. No advertising. No tracking across apps or websites. No cookies, no device fingerprinting. No sale or sharing of data, because none is collected.

The app's App Store privacy label is **Data Not Collected**.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing held about you to access, correct or delete.

You are in control of what is on your device: revoke camera or location access at any time in iOS Settings, delete individual pins, or parcels with their pages, pins and photos, inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.
